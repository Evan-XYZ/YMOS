# Strategy family map

> These cards are diagnostic coordinates, not recommendations or executable defaults. No family supplies public parameter values.

| Family | Primary evidence | Typical invalidation authority | Cadence tendency | Common structural conflict |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Value / mean reversion | Estimated value, balance-sheet or normalized earnings evidence | Value thesis impairment, changed assumptions, time/capital constraints | Lower frequency | Calling price decline “more value” without rechecking the thesis |
| Growth / quality | Durable growth, unit economics, competitive position, execution | Deteriorating economics, broken growth driver, capital-allocation change | Earnings/operating cadence | Using a long-duration story to excuse permanent deterioration |
| Trend / momentum | Relative strength, persistence, liquidity and price behavior | Defined trend break, failed continuation, regime change | Higher frequency | Entering on trend, then switching to fundamentals after the trend fails |
| Event-driven | Identified catalyst, probability tree, timetable, payoff structure | Event failure, delay, changed terms, probability update | Event timetable | Removing the deadline after the catalyst passes |
| Short-horizon / microstructure | Order flow, liquidity, volatility, intraday or short-cycle setup | Setup invalidation, time stop, liquidity change | High frequency | Converting a short-horizon trade into an unplanned long-term hold |
| Relative value / arbitrage | Spread, hedge relationship, convergence mechanism | Relationship break, financing/borrow change, model error | Model/event dependent | Treating gross spread as profit while ignoring basis and execution risk |

## Mixing families

Mixing is allowed. Require an explicit answer to:

1. Which family supplies the entry judge?
2. Which family has invalidation authority?
3. Which family determines sizing and horizon?
4. What happens when judges disagree?

If the user cannot answer, classify the mix as an unresolved kernel conflict rather than choosing for them.

## What this map does not contain

- Security-selection advice.
- Position-size or stop defaults.
- Claims that one family is superior.
- A rule that every investor must fit exactly one family.
